How to read these results

Two LightGBM models and local ridge regression are combined with fixed weights of 37.5%, 37.5% and 25%. Each historical block uses its own earlier training window. These values are not the competition's final score, or an evaluation of the final 1993–2022 fit on earlier years.

Global RMSE pools squared errors rather than averaging yearly RMSE. Development years have already been consulted; they do not establish performance in future years.

Forecast details and sources

Issued: 30 September 2026, 18:18 UTC. First verification eligible from 1 February 2027, using final ERA5. No observed October rainfall or accuracy score is included.

The registered model uses two LightGBM models and local ridge regression, fitted on 1993–2022 with weights of 37.5%, 37.5% and 25%. Atmospheric inputs use T−4, ocean indices T−3 and seasonal initializations T−1. Required sources passed their checks before issuance.

Local receipt times and file hashes are preserved in the registry; they are not an independent external timestamp. Coastline: Natural Earth; used only for display.

How to read these results

These metrics come from earlier training windows evaluated on 2007–2020 with longer input lags. They do not score the October forecast or evaluate the final 1993–2022 fit on earlier years. Global RMSE pools squared errors.

The source-lag protocol differs from the competition model, so the two tabs are not a controlled paired comparison. Development years have already been consulted. Historical improvements do not establish future skill. U-Net and global SST/PCA remain separate experiments.
