COMPETITION · 8G

The submitted hybrid

Two LightGBM models and local ridge regression, combined with fixed weights of 37.5%, 37.5% and 25%. The final fit used 1993–2022 to predict 2023–2024.

The metrics below are the archived seven-block validation over 2007–2020. They are not the competition's 2024 score or the performance of a model fitted through 2022 on earlier years.

CURRENT REFERENCE

The hybrid with longer input lags

The architecture and fixed blend are retained. Atmospheric inputs use T−4, ocean indices T−3, and seasonal initializations T−1. The operational package was fitted separately on 1993–2022; U-Net and global SST/PCA remain research experiments.

These historical metrics come from earlier training windows evaluated on 2007–2020. They use the lagged protocol and are not a paired new-model contest against the competition tab. Development years have already been consulted.
